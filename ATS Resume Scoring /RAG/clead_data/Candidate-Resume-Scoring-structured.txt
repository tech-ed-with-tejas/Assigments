 Introduction
-  Getting a bunch of candidates for your open job positions leads to a large amount of time being wasted on checking their resumes. Such a bottleneck is not acceptable if you want to ease out the process. Automating resume screening is one of the best options in such a case. What is resume screening? Well, it is the process of identifying what qualities a candidate consists of and then scoring them based on these skills that they consist of.
-  This allows the recruiters to get a clear view of whether the candidate is eligible for the said position. Especially when there is a lot of information being provided in the resume finding that one essential factor which will qualify the candidate is important. This can now be automated with the resume screening procedure.
-  Resume screening is also said to be a part of the candidate screening procedure. With multiple stages being involved in the candidate screening, it is essential to remove as many bottlenecks as possible and change the process to an efficient one. Resume screening hence proves to be a crucial step to determine whether the candidate can move on to the next stage of interviewing. This method increases efficiency especially when high-volume applications are being sent in.
-  The screening will help search for three parts of a resume such as:
-  Must-haves are the utmost important skills, whereas happy-to-haves are those that will add-on to the efficiency of the position and the impressions such as a candidate’s communication and team-building skills will also play a part in this selection.
